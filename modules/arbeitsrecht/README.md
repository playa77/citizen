# Arbeitsrecht backend package (WP-132)

Module 0.2.0 / core 3.7.0 / module API 3.5. Experimental employee-side
individual employment law for your own matter. Human legal and language
review remain deferred under D-071. No sending, filing, deployment ingestion
or live inference quality claim.

The dismissal rule needs confirmed actual receipt, a written employer
dismissal and confirmation that no administrative-consent reservation applies.
Unknown qualifications remain missing input. The critical warning remains
important for small employers and short service. Special protection requires
urgent individual review, never a reassuring guessed deadline.

Jobseeker registration separates `kenntnis_datum` from actual receipt and
`ereignis_datum` (termination). Confirm the applicable branch explicitly.
Dates are preliminary planning aids. Contact the employment agency promptly;
special procedural conditions and impediments need individual assessment.
The backwards three-month target requires a qualified confirmation that the
target is a working day (`meldeziel_werktag_bestaetigt`); otherwise no date
is given. § 26 SGB X procedural exceptions remain individually reviewable.

Contractual/tariff exclusion periods have no generic automatic validity,
stage or duration check. Collect the actual clauses and evidence. Drafts for
wages, reference correction and court preparation retain `[BITTE PRÜFEN]`.
A severance estimate never advises allowing a dismissal deadline to expire;
termination agreements require advice about possible Sperrzeit consequences.

Calculators require a confirmed applicability assessment and date-selected
parameters. Unsupported regimes and incomplete facts produce review needs.
See `parameters/` and `sources/` for provisional values and dated evidence.
The corpus mapping declares ten federal source types; it proves neither
successful deployment ingestion nor comprehensive retrieval coverage.

The 18 synthetic cases include missing receipt/knowledge, short service,
small employers, administrative-consent uncertainty, weekend and leap-year
boundaries, exclusion clauses, leave, severance, references and agreements.
Dossier/plain fixtures contain literal hand-derived dates rather than dates
created by the production evaluator. Offline evaluation checks deterministic
contracts, not live model quality or human legal approval.
