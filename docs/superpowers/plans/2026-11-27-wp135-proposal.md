# WP-135 — experimental Familienrecht backend proposal

Status: approved by owner on 2026-11-27: “approved. proceed.”
Baseline: merged WP-134, `main` / `origin/main` at `db57937` (2026-11-27).

## Outcome and boundaries

Build out M6 `familienrecht` through the existing module framework for people
preparing their own separation, maintenance, family-court and maintenance-advance
matters. Custody/contact and child-welfare topics remain information and referral
first. Do not generate custody recommendations, assess parental fitness or
promise a maintenance entitlement or court outcome.

Deliver the nine roadmap subpackages: dated primary-source verification,
versioned proposed parameters, qualified deterministic dates and calculators,
guarded preparation drafts, intake schema, dossier, DE/EN/de-LS copy, at least
15 synthetic cases and offline evaluation reports, and compliance notices.
Keep experimental maturity and proposed/draft review status under D-071.
Minimise children's and former partners' data; prefer ages and abstract
identifiers to names, exact birth dates and sensitive personal narratives.

## Specific architectural changes proposed

1. Add FamFG, UVG and Mindestunterhaltsverordnung source types through the
   existing federal corpus adapter, catalog, citation/retrieval mappings and
   PostgreSQL source-type constraint. Extend the single Alembic chain with a
   guarded downgrade. Reuse BGB and supporting ZPO sources. Verify the official
   ordinance identity and parser coverage before finalising identifiers.
2. Add a narrowly scoped shared FamFG procedural rollover entry using the
   existing current civil calendar and confirmed local holidays. Use existing
   explicit-service/event qualification contracts for supported complaint
   dates. Keep document applicability, ordinary versus special proceedings and
   legal bases in module data; do not redesign the deadline API. Verify the
   statutory chain before implementation, and test the resulting calendar rule.

No new service, dependency, database, API workflow, default module or frontend.
Further material shared extensions require a separate specific proposal.
Update core/module capability versions, API documentation and regressions where
these additive capabilities affect contracts.

## Approaches and consequences

Recommended: extend the established corpus and deadline libraries as above.
This provides retrievable statutes and reusable procedural mechanics while
keeping family-law rules in the module. It requires a migration, source identity
and parser tests, deadline golden cases and integration/provenance checks.

Alternative: module-only static sources and calculator-local dates. This avoids
shared extensions but leaves statutory retrieval incomplete and duplicates
calendar mechanics. A separate family-procedure service would add infrastructure
without a demonstrated need and is not recommended.

## Legal and calculation limits

Verify current official statutes and dated values before encoding rules. Bound
source requests to one in flight per publisher, at least two seconds between
requests, at most three attempts per resource and ten minutes per publisher;
honour longer Retry-After periods and stop on persistent denial. No embeddings,
deployment ingestion or live inference.

Implement cent-exact, parameter-backed statutory minimum-maintenance,
child-benefit credit and UVG arithmetic only for explicitly qualified regimes.
Separate arithmetic from eligibility, income assessment, ranking, shared care,
adult-child treatment and enforceability. Missing facts or unsupported periods
produce review/unavailable outcomes. Spousal maintenance and self-retention
remain preparation/review guidance where no supported parameter regime exists.

Investigate OQ-7 using publisher material. Do not bundle the Düsseldorfer Tabelle
or guideline/self-retention data without established reuse terms. If unresolved,
record the limitation and provide statutory calculations plus an external
guideline reference; do not claim a complete table or full maintenance award.
Treat retroactive-maintenance dates as qualified statutory conditions rather
than inventing a filing deadline. Distinguish ordinary complaints from urgent
and other special proceedings; unsupported regimes require prompt advice.

Drafts cover information requests, a response outline and UVG application
preparation, with `[BITTE PRÜFEN]` for unconfirmed material. No automatic filing,
messages to third parties or personalised child-welfare adjudication.

eligibility facts, complaint scope, confirmed service versus issue dates,
month-end/weekend/state/local holiday rollover and special-procedure refusals.
Test guarded drafts, minimisation copy, intake/dossier, source identity and
disclosure/provenance. Run focused regressions, all-module validation, offline
dossier/plain evaluations, purpose drift and applicable CI gates using the
existing environment and disposable PostgreSQL 16/pgvector.

Record approved architecture in D-077 and execution/results in the implementation
