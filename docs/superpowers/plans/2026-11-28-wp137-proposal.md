# WP-137 — experimental Steuerrecht backend proposal

Status: approved by owner in this Codex chat: “approved. proceed.”
Baseline: merged WP-136, `origin/main` at `598f68d`, 2026-11-28.

## Outcome and boundaries

Implement M8 `steuerrecht` for natural persons preparing their own income-tax
matters: understanding assessments and tax-office requests, comparing declared
and assessed amounts, preparing objections and requests concerning enforcement
or simple amendment. Deliver roadmap subpackages .1–.9 through existing module
interfaces: dated statutory sources, proposed versioned parameters, deterministic
checks, guarded drafts, minimised intake/dossier, DE/EN/de-LS static copy, at least
15 synthetic cases, offline evaluation reports and compliance notices.

Keep experimental maturity, proposed parameters and draft copy. D-071 defers
human legal/language review and promotion; it does not establish completed review.
Third-party assistance and advice-center operation are unsupported. No frontend,
automatic filing, tax-return submission, deployment ingestion, live inference,
new service or dependency. Do not embed gettsim or other AGPL code (D-041).

## Specific shared changes requiring approval

1. Extend the existing federal corpus adapter/catalog, citation and retrieval
   mappings with EStG, AO, SolZG, FGO and StBerG. Add source types through the
   single PostgreSQL migration chain with a guarded downgrade preserving stored
   sources. These sources support the module's substantive and procedural scope
   and its own-matter boundary. Verify official identities before encoding them.
2. Add AO-specific deadline rollover and notification-fiction entries to the existing procedural date library,
   reusing its civil calendar and explicit confirmed facts. Implement qualified
   domestic notification presumptions and objection/defective-instruction periods
   through existing deadline/calculator interfaces. Distinguish dispatch,
   effective notification and document issue dates; never treat an assessment's
   issue date as dispatch evidence. Keep filing-year and transitional rules in
   versioned module data; unsupported service modes, disputed receipt and
   unconfirmed regimes yield no definitive date. The tax fiction must reject
   missing dispatch evidence rather than inherit the legacy issue-date fallback;
   distinguish early receipt from later/disputed receipt rather than unconditionally
   replacing presumed notification with any actual receipt date. Authorize narrowly
   scoped AO evaluator safeguards with direct-engine and dossier regressions;
   preserve other modules' behavior. No new endpoint or generic
   deadline fact model is proposed. If existing interfaces cannot safely express
   necessary facts, return with a separate specific proposal before changing them.
3. Add optional `scope.third_party_assistance` manifest metadata and expose it
   through the existing catalog. Require it to be false for Steuerrecht, matching
   the module catalog. Existing modules may omit it and retain their behavior.
   Reuse the current own-matter intake validation and purpose safeguards; do not
   invent a new account/role system or imply metadata alone enforces who uses
   the backend. Document the supported-use boundary and test its validation.

Update shared capability versions, API documentation and contract regressions
where these additions apply. No module defaults or selection changes. Any
further material architectural change requires separate specific approval.

## Approaches and consequences

Recommended: extend the existing corpus, procedural calendar and manifest
interfaces as above. This retains one retrieval system and deadline mechanism,
supports primary-source evidence and makes the tax module's assistance boundary
machine-readable. It requires a migration, additive catalog metadata and focused
source, calendar, schema and compatibility tests.

Alternative: module-only references and calculator-local date handling. This
reduces shared edits but leaves retrieval incomplete and duplicates procedural
rules; it still needs manifest support to fulfil the catalog requirement.
A separate tax service adds infrastructure without a demonstrated need.

## Legal and calculation limits

Verify current official statutory text before implementing values or periods.
Publisher verification is limited to one request in flight per publisher, at
least two seconds between requests, at most three attempts per resource and ten
minutes per publisher, honoring longer Retry-After and stopping on persistent
denial. No corpus ingestion or embeddings during verification.

Implement law-derived income-tax tariff arithmetic using exact decimal/integer
operations and versioned tax-year parameters. Clearly distinguish taxable income
from gross income and a tariff check from a complete tax assessment. Parameterise
supported allowances and filing deadlines by year; publish a coverage table of
supported tax years, allowances and filing regimes bounded by verified sources.
Check the existing rollover library's AO commentary against those sources rather
than treating it as authority. Refuse unsupported years or
special regimes instead of extrapolating. Compare explicitly supplied assessment
and declaration values without asserting entitlement to a deduction. Exclude
business-tax, international and other unsupported specialist regimes.

Drafts cover Einspruch, Aussetzung der Vollziehung and schlichte Änderung,
with `[BITTE PRÜFEN]` for unconfirmed facts. Explain that an objection/request
does not by itself establish suspension or preserve every other remedy. Do not
invent automatic extensions or assurances of outcome. Minimise financial intake;
do not require tax IDs, bank details or third-party identifiers for arithmetic.
