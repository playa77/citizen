# WP-132 — proposed employment-law implementation

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-27: “approved. proceed.”
Baseline: merged WP-131, `origin/main` at `1aaf606` (2026-11-27).

## Outcome and boundaries

Implement employee-side individual employment law from catalog M3 through the
existing module framework: dismissal, warnings, wages, leave, employment
references and termination agreements. Keep the module experimental, with
draft/proposed review metadata under D-071. No frontend, publication, deployment,
live inference or automatic sending. Preserve the owner's local AGENTS.md edit.

Deliver the nine roadmap subpackages: dated source verification, versioned
parameters, deterministic deadlines and calculators, guarded drafts, public
intake schema, dossier, DE/EN/de-LS copy, at least 15 synthetic goldset cases,
offline reports and compliance notices. Actual deployment ingestion and live
pipeline quality remain separate from offline implementation verification.

## Specific shared extensions proposed for approval

1. Enable official federal ingestion for existing `kschg` and `burlg` source
   types; add `tzbfg`, `entgfg`, `milog`, `muschg`, `arbzg` and `arbgg` through
   the existing corpus catalog/parser and PostgreSQL source constraint. Extend
   the single Alembic chain, retaining a guarded downgrade when new-source
   rows exist. BGB and SGB III reuse existing sources. No new service,
   dependency, database or source-selection default.
2. Make the already declared but currently rejected `kenntnis` deadline
   trigger executable. Add an explicit confirmed knowledge date to the existing
   engine/dossier fact contract, separate from receipt, letter and termination
   dates. Use it for the short-notice branch of § 38 SGB III; use the existing
   backwards event-date path for the three-month branch. Module-owned explicit
   qualifications select the appropriate branch; missing or uncertain facts
   produce missing-input results rather than guessed dates. Verify applicable
   calendar and rollover semantics against primary sources before encoding
   either branch. Preserve existing administrative, receipt and service paths.

The § 4 KSchG dismissal deadline uses existing explicit receipt support,
21 calendar days and `critical` severity in the mandatory dossier banner.
Special administrative-consent cases must remain explicitly guarded; neither
short tenure nor small employer size may silently suppress the warning.
Contractual/tariff exclusion clauses remain evidence-bound review items:
do not infer their validity, stages or duration from a generic statutory rule.

## Module implementation within existing extension points

- Pure, date-qualified, parameter-backed calculators for § 622 BGB notice,
  § 1a KSchG severance, BUrlG leave/compensation and minimum-wage comparisons.
  Require confirmed applicability and facts; preserve exact money arithmetic
  and explain rounding. Refuse unsupported special regimes rather than
  inventing a statutory entitlement. A § 1a estimate must never recommend
  letting a dismissal deadline expire.
- Guarded wage-claim and reference-correction drafts, plus preparation for the
  Rechtsantragstelle. Unconfirmed material retains `[BITTE PRÜFEN]` slots.
- Intake, dossier and static copy cover urgent dismissal review, contractual
  exclusion clauses, special protections and Sperrzeit risks of termination
  agreements without deciding benefit sanctions or recommending signature.
- Update disclaimer addenda/version, prompt version and pinned expectations
  deliberately; record module SemVer, source coverage and release notes.

## Alternatives and consequences

Recommended: the two additive shared extensions above. They retain current
ownership boundaries and make employment sources and knowledge-based deadlines
available through the established interfaces. They require a database migration,
additive API documentation and shared deadline/corpus regression checks.

Alternative: a module-only subset with static Arbeitsuchendmeldung warnings and
no new corpus types. This avoids shared changes but leaves roadmap .1 and .3
incomplete. Encoding knowledge as receipt or putting employment-specific rules
in core would obscure legal semantics and is not recommended.

## Preliminary primary-source findings

These are reconnaissance, not completed statutory verification:

- [§ 38 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__38.html): the
  short-notice branch runs from knowledge of the termination date, requiring
  a distinct trigger from receipt.
- [§ 1a KSchG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__1a.html): the
  statutory severance has specific prerequisites; more than six months of a
  remaining employment year rounds up, rather than every fraction of a year.
- [§ 5 KSchG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__5.html): late
  admission is a qualified exception, not an automatic extension.
