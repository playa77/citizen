# WP-136 — experimental Aufenthaltsrecht backend proposal

Status: approved by owner in this Codex chat: “approved. proceed.”
Baseline: merged WP-135, main/origin main `1daf5d8`, 2026-11-27.

## Outcome and boundaries

Implement M7 `aufenthaltsrecht` for people preparing their own residence-title,
asylum-procedure and naturalisation matters. Deliver roadmap subpackages .1–.9
through the existing module interfaces: dated source verification, proposed
versioned parameters, qualified deterministic checks, guarded preparation drafts,
minimised intake/dossier, multilingual static copy, at least 15 synthetic cases,
offline evaluation reports and compliance notices. Keep experimental maturity
and draft review status; D-071 defers human review and promotion.

Asylum is information, deadline protection and urgent referral only. Do not
write asylum narratives, assess credibility, predict protection decisions or
serve authority-side migration assessment. Avoid collecting persecution,
health, religion or other sensitive narratives. No frontend work, automatic
filing, deployment ingestion or live inference is included.

## Specific architectural changes proposed

1. Extend the existing federal corpus adapter, catalog, citation and retrieval
   mappings with AufenthG, AsylG, AufenthV, BeschV, FreizügG/EU, StAG and VwGO.
   Add their source types to PostgreSQL through the single Alembic chain, with
   a guarded downgrade. Reuse existing VwVfG, BGB, ZPO and social-law sources.
   Verify official identifiers and parser coverage before encoding them.
2. Add VwGO procedural rollover to the existing deadline library, using the
   current civil calendar and confirmed local holidays. Reuse explicit service,
   document-type and confirmed-fact qualification; preserve existing HTTP/SSE
   contracts. Keep applicable procedure, deadline length and exclusions in
   module data. Unsupported asylum special regimes must refuse a definitive
   date and retain an urgent referral warning.
3. Extend the existing static plain-language content with AR, FA, UK, TR and RU
   alongside DE, EN and DE-LS, including core disclaimer/AI/privacy summaries
   needed to consume the module in those languages. Reuse existing language
   metadata and ISO-date fallback for these languages; no translation service,
   dependency or dynamic translation. Add regression/evaluation coverage for
   every language. Backend delivery does not claim GUI accessibility, RTL
   rendering, integration or P12 usability acceptance.

No new service, database, inference path, module default or dependency. Further
material shared changes require a separate specific proposal. Update capability
versions and API documentation where shared capabilities change.

## Approaches, rationale and consequences

Recommended: extend established corpus, deadline and static-copy libraries as
above. This supports retrievable primary law and consistent procedural dates
without duplicating mechanisms. It requires a migration, source/parser tests,
calendar regressions and multilingual content checks.

Alternative: module-only references and calculator-local dates. This reduces
shared edits but leaves retrieval incomplete and duplicates procedural handling.
A separate migration-law service or translation service adds infrastructure and
privacy exposure without a demonstrated need and is not recommended.

## Legal and calculation limits

Verify current official law before implementing any rule or value. Bound
publisher traffic to one request in flight per publisher, at least two seconds
between requests, at most three attempts per resource and ten minutes per
publisher; honour longer Retry-After and stop on persistent denial. No embeddings
or database ingestion during source verification.

Support explicitly qualified ordinary administrative/asylum deadlines; distinguish
one-month, two-week and one-week regimes and interim relief. Never infer a
special regime from a generic decision label. State-law objection availability
must be evidence-backed and versioned; unknown coverage remains unavailable.

Treat timely residence-title applications and fiction effects as conditional
statutory checks, not promises of continued status or travel/work permission.
Naturalisation residence-period arithmetic is not eligibility adjudication;
exclude unsupported residence-credit and transition regimes. Livelihood checks
are bounded arithmetic using explicit inputs and versioned module parameters,
not an automatic eligibility result or an implicit load of Sozialrecht.

Drafts are extension/application checklists, file-access preparation and requests
for extension only where legally extendable; never suggest statutory court
filing periods can be extended by an informal request. Unconfirmed material
retains `[BITTE PRÜFEN]` markers. Urgent advice warnings persist when facts are
missing or dates cannot be calculated.

refusals, month ends, weekends, state/local holidays, application/expiry boundaries,
unsupported periods, exact arithmetic and absent eligibility facts. Verify

provenance and every supplied language. Run focused regressions, all-module
validation, deterministic dossier/plain evaluation, purpose drift and applicable
